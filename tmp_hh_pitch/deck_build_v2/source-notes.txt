Editorial decisions introduced in this deck
- Added a six-slide worldview chapter that separates philosophy from executional device.
- Defined the worldview as: 世界不是用来征服的，是用来进入的 / The world is not to be conquered, but entered.
- Established the complete chain: Friluftsliv (relationship with the world) + Trust by Professional (HH method) -> Stay & Feel Alive (human outcome) -> 向更远 赴新域 (annual expression).
- Established five worldview laws that govern every later creative decision.
- Established a five-symbol system: wind, crew, gear, light and logbook.
- Reclassified THE LOGBOOK from Big Idea to Narrative Device: the worldview is the belief system; the logbook makes it visible and renewable.
- Reframed the Logbook as a new 150th-anniversary platform that begins now and can be renewed annually, rather than an unverified artifact passed down since 1877.
- Kept Friluftsliv as a cultural design principle, not a consumer-facing campaign headline.
- Reframed the ambassador as the audience's first crew member: visible and emotionally useful, but not a solo hero who overwhelms the brand.
- Replaced surrender-to-uncertainty language with professional trust: expertise, crew and equipment make the unknown reachable.
- Unified the two films as Chapter 01 / Chapter 02 of one platform while preserving their distinct communication jobs.
